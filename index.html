How it works

Four months, then it's yours

  1. Month 1

    Map

    An online call to see where your time goes. We choose the two or three tasks worth automating first.

  2. Month 2

    Set up

    I set up the assistant, the automations or the website on the tools you already use.

  3. Month 3

    Hand over

    You get written guides and templates, and we go through them together until you can adjust things yourself.

  4. Month 4

    Run it yourself

    You run it on your own. One check-in call to fix what isn't working, then you're independent.
